# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Captured from the grilling session with David on 2026-11-03 (16 questions, all answered). Nothing here is inferred without his answer.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain HTML, CSS and ES modules on Node 20+ with zero dependencies. `npm start` and `npm test` work without `npm install`. Deployed to Vercel from GitHub.

## Users

Dani, 17, a high school senior on her phone between classes. She likes hands-on work, is torn between the Northline Electrical Apprenticeship and community college, and wants to know what to do before winter break.

Buyers: state agencies and school districts piloting with several thousand students, who need proof that nothing is invented and nothing about a minor reaches the model.

## Product Purpose

Move one person to one concrete next step with a date on it, and make every claim checkable. Success is Dani leaving with a calendar date, a saved plan or an advisor request code.

## Positioning

Code decides, the model only words it, and a person backs up everything the records can't confirm. Every fact shows its source record and the date it was last checked.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Only the eight synthetic records may be stated. Seats, one person's eligibility, exact aid amounts and unlisted dates are unknown and go to the advisor hand-off.
- Never "eligible", "accepted", "enrolled", "approved" or "awarded". A saved plan is never an application.
- Her name and typed text never reach the model. The name is filled in on the device.
- The model is a scripted stand-in, labeled as such.

## Brand Commitments

Pathways by CampusEvolve (the challenge's product). Visual language pinned by David: Mano's palette and warmth, Front Door's structure for answers, sources and steps. Light mode only.

## Evidence on Hand

The eight records in `src/core/records.js`. No testimonials, outcomes or usage numbers exist and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Grounded or silent.
2. One next step, with a date, before anything else.
3. A person is always one tap away.
4. Kind and plain. Talk to a teenager like a capable adult.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Minors are the primary users. Tap targets at least 44px, contrast at least 4.5:1, status always in words as well as color, eighth-grade reading level, crisis lines (988, 911) always reachable.
