How this works

Pathways says only what these records say. A person backs up everything they don’t, and nothing about a student reaches the model.

Code decides

Deadlines, the age rule, what’s unknown and when to send someone to an advisor are decided in planner.js. The model only writes two or three warm sentences, and code checks every number, date and citation before they’re shown. A reply that fails is replaced with plain text built by code.

The model never sees the student

Every model call is built from a closed list: record IDs, decision codes, topic codes and the exact wording of record facts. gateway.js refuses anything else. The student’s name is filled in on their phone after the reply comes back. Typed questions are matched to a topic on the phone, and only the topic is sent.

A person backs it up

Seats, one person’s eligibility, exact aid amounts and dates the records don’t list always get “I can’t confirm that” and the advisor hand-off. Crisis words show 988 and 911 right away, with no model involved.
